01 · Requirement Analysis Docket

Turn brief statements into numbered, testable obligations and explicit exclusions.

The question

What problem are we solving? What is in scope, out of scope, assumed, or still unclear?

Talentra example

Legacy candidate, job and application records are inconsistent. The full-scale design target is 10 million candidate profiles; the laptop proof target is 1 million.

What to show

18 grouped REQ IDs, four OS exclusions, source and priority, actor links, ambiguity notes, and ranked questions.

Docket sentence→REQ or OS ID→Acceptance intent

Review gap: Source blocks are indexed, but a sentence-by-sentence and B1–B82 audit remains open.

02 · Stakeholders, Personas & Roles

Name every human and system actor, then define each actor's goal, frequency, permission and denial.

The question

Who uses the system, who governs it, and who is affected when data or access changes?

Talentra example

A recruiter may contact candidates only for assigned jobs in their own company. A candidate's block must hide that candidate from the blocked party.

What to show

16 actor IDs, persona cards and a role-permission matrix, including auditor, support staff, regulator, scheduled worker and database engineer.

REQ-03 / REQ-04→Recruiter / candidate→Allow or deny + audit

Review gap: The matrix is proposed policy. TPO, guest, support and elevated-admin access still need owner review and role-based tests.

03 · Feature Discovery

Prioritize capabilities and compare documented competitor coverage without overstating evidence.

The question

Which features are must-have, should-have or potential differentiators? Which requirement supports each one?

Talentra example

Candidate profiles, jobs and applications are core. A connected gig handoff and financial event trail is a proposed differentiation opportunity.

What to show

12 FEAT IDs and a public-source comparison of Naukri, Monster and LinkedIn.

REQ ID→FEAT ID + priority→Competitor evidence

Review gap: Public pages do not prove a competitor lacks an internal capability; a field-level comparison is still needed.

04 · NFR Discovery

Write each quality target as a number with a workload, measurement method and evidence artifact.

The question

What scale, latency, availability, security, privacy, compliance and accessibility must the design support?

Talentra example

The local seed has 1m candidates, 100k jobs, 5m applications. A 60 reads/s load and 500 ms p95 lookup are proposals based on explicit, unverified activity assumptions.

What to show

13 NFR IDs, tests and evidence status; DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025 mapping, and proposed WCAG 2.2 AA for a future UI.

Volume assumption→Numeric target→Test result

Review gap: Service targets need approval; per-entity personal-data retention periods remain undecided. The proposed 24-month candidate review is not an approved deletion rule. Local seed counts do not prove production performance.

05 · Business Workflow Analysis

Trace each must-have capability from trigger through decisions to output and exception handling.

The question

Who starts the work, what decisions happen, what is recorded, and how does the system recover?

Talentra example

For a subscription payment: confirm once, append a ledger event, and use a compensating entry for a refund. A timeout moves to reconciliation.

What to show

12 WF IDs, workflow diagrams and an exception catalogue covering cancel, refund, retry and timeout.

Trigger→Decision→Output↘Exception / recovery

Review gap: Domain owners still need to approve exception ownership, state transitions and reconciliation behavior.
